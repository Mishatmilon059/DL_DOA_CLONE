# Agent 04 - Mathematical Bug Hunter: IABR-Net v2 dataset generation

Scope: dataset-generation operations only (`iabr2_sim.py`, `make_bank` / bank loading in `notebook_src.py`, the v1 bank builders, the frozen `eval_bank.npz`). Model accuracy is out of scope. No project file was modified; all checks are scripts in `IABR_v2_DATASET_AUDIT/scratch/` (`a04_01_core.py`, `a04_02_seeds.py`, `a04_03_heat.py`, `a04_04_eval.py`, `a04_05_banks.py`, `a04_06_points.py`). Labels: FACT / DERIVATION / INFERENCE / ASSUMPTION / CONCERN / VERIFIED BUG. Confidence scale: Confirmed bug / Strong concern / Possible issue / No issue found.

## 0. Executive summary

* **No numerical or mathematical bug was found in the signal model, codebooks, impairment model, noise scaling, heat-target construction or up/down-sampling maps.** Each was checked against an independent implementation (the project's original generator `DG`, closed forms, brute force loops) and agreed to machine precision or to the stated tolerance.
* **One confirmed data-design defect (seed collision):** three groups of v1 banks share a seed. `gain_g2_snr0` and `gain_g0.5_snr15` (seed 2020) are the *same 500 scenes with the same noise realisation and the same gain-ripple shape*, differing only in scalar scale factors. This is a verified statistical dependence, not a bug in the physics; effect on results is low. The other collisions (`phase_d0_snr{0,15}` vs `nuis_*`) share only the first scene.
* Everything else is either inherited from the original DL-DOA protocol (angle sampler uniform in angle, not in cos; per-entry SNR definition) or a modelling limitation.
* Items in the task list that turned out to be **No issue found** are listed in section 4 with the evidence.

## 2. Numerical verification results (all VERIFIED by running code)

### V1. Codebooks (FACT, verified)
$F_{mp}=\tfrac14e^{-j2\pi mp/16}$, $W_{nq}=\tfrac14e^{+j2\pi nq/16}$; $\max|M^HM-I|=2.4\times10^{-15}$, closed-form deviation $\le3.6\times10^{-15}$. Unitary, so $\|Y\|_F=\|H\|_F$ without noise (difference $2.8\times10^{-12}$). With these, $Y_{qp}=\sum_l\alpha_l\sqrt{NtNr}\,[W^Ha_r]_q[a_t^{H}F]_p$ is peaked where $-\pi\cos\psi\equiv 2\pi q/16$ and $\pi\cos\phi\equiv2\pi p/16$ (mod $2\pi$). This is the convention used by `angles_to_cells`.

### V2. `simulate` vs original generator (FACT, verified)
For $L\in\{1,3,5,9\}$ padded to `lmax=9`, $Y$ from `simulate` vs `(W^H H_{DG}) F + Z` using `DG.generate_channel_v2`: max error $3.6\times10^{-15}$. Padded and unpadded $H$ are identical (assert $<10^{-13}$). No NaN in $H$ or $Y$: the `nan_to_num` angle 0 gives a valid steering vector, and $\alpha=0$ removes its contribution exactly (multiplying by 0, not NaN).

### V3. Impairment model (FACT, verified)
`error_deg` in the original DG multiplies the codebooks by a per-antenna phase, identical over beams (deviation $2\times10^{-16}$). Feeding that phase through the v2 formula gives $|Y_{DG}-Y_{v2}|_{\max}=2.7\times10^{-15}$. Empirical draws: phase range $\pm5^\circ$, std $2.886^\circ$ (theory $2.887^\circ$); gain $\pm3$ dB, std $1.733$ dB (theory $1.732$). Note the original draws $\epsilon\in U(-d,d)$ per antenna on the codebook, so v2 with $\delta=d$ is the same distribution; the original has no gain error (v2 extension).

### V4. Noise / SNR (FACT, verified)
$E|z|^2=0.10003$ at 10 dB (target $0.1$); Re/Im variances $0.05003/0.04999$; correlation $-1.6\times10^{-4}$. `DG.generate_noise`: $0.10007$. So v2 noise scaling equals the original. Signal power per entry (noise-free, no impairment): $L=1$: $0.9958$, $L=3$: $0.9988$, $L=9$: $0.9933$ (equal to the sample mean of $\sum|\alpha|^2$). Hence "SNR" is the *per-entry* SNR with expected signal power $\approx1$, not the per-sample realised SNR: the per-sample signal power has std $0.99$ (L=1), $0.58$ (L=3), $0.34$ (L=9) about the mean. This is the paper's definition; label as **not a bug** but note that the realised SNR of a given sample varies strongly at small $L$ (CONCERN for interpretation only).

### V5. Peak cell vs `angles_to_cells` (FACT, verified)
3000 noise-free single-path samples: `argmax|Y|` within 0.5 cell (circular) of the continuous coordinate for all 3000, max deviation $0.49993$. `H = W_id Y F_id^H` recovers $H$ to $1.4\times10^{-14}$. So rows = AoA via $-\pi\cos\psi$ and cols = AoD via $+\pi\cos\phi$ are correct, and the sign conventions on the two axes are opposite by construction.

### V6. Impairment sign convention and projector (FACT, verified)
$\mathrm{to\_ant}(Y)=W_0YF_0^H=\bar D_r H D_t$ with error $1.9\times10^{-14}$ (with $\delta=8^\circ$, 3 dB). Target rows use $-\angle D_r$ and columns $+\angle D_t$ - consistent (max deviation $7\times10^{-9}$, float32 cast). Log-gain rows/cols match after mean removal ($1.5\times10^{-8}$). The projector removes mean and linear ramp (mean $4\times10^{-17}$, slope $5\times10^{-15}$, idempotent to $2\times10^{-16}$); the residual after subtracting the target is an exact affine function of the antenna index ($5.6\times10^{-17}$). `np.angle` of the $\le8^\circ$ ramp never wraps, so there is no phase-wrapping error in the targets (DERIVATION: the maximum phase magnitude is $\delta_{\max}$ in radians $\ll\pi$).

### V7. Heat targets (FACT, verified)
Compared to a brute-force loop on 150 scenes ($L$ up to 9): max difference $9\times10^{-9}$ (float32). No NaN. Padded slots are masked: without the mask, a padded slot (`psi=phi=0`) would light cell $(16,16)$ of the 32-grid; the mask is present and `NaN(psi)` mask equals `NaN(phi)` mask. `np.round` half-to-even is irrelevant (0 exact $.5$ cases among 1500 scenes; in continuous angles the probability is 0). Positives ($\ge1-10^{-6}$) equal $L$ in 97.7 % of scenes; they are *fewer than $L$* when two paths round to the same 32-grid cell: fraction by $L$: 3: 1.3 %, 4: 0.6 %, 5: 2.9 %, 6: 2.3 %, 7: 7.4 %, 8: 4.2 %, 9: 2.2 %; never more than $L$. Consequence: with a focal-loss positive criterion of "target $\ge1-10^{-6}$", ~1-7 % of high-$L$ scenes have fewer positives than paths. That is an inherent resolution limit of a 32-grid, not a bug (Possible issue for high-$L$ training only).

### V8. Up/down-sampling maps (FACT, verified)
`UP_SRC` equals `scipy.ndimage.zoom(...,4,order=0)` exactly. `DOWN_IDX` covers all 256 source cells; `UP_SRC[DOWN_IDX]==arange(256)`; `downsample16(upsample64(y))` is exact. The zoom index is $\mathrm{round}(o\cdot15/63)$, *not* $\lfloor o/4\rfloor$: source-row counts are 3,4,4,4,4,5,4,...,4,5,4,4,4,4,3 (cells 5 and 10 occupy 5 output rows; cells 0 and 15 only 3). This is a property of the *original* dataset (and `eval_bank` is a zoom-4 of a 16x16 observation, asserted in `infer_dldoa_test.py:20`), not of v2. Because the 64x64 data is exactly piecewise-constant, `DOWN_IDX` (first occurrence of each source) recovers the 16x16 losslessly. No bug; but any model that treats the 64x64 image as a smoothly-sampled field would see a slightly nonuniform 3/4/5 block structure (stylistic/INFERENCE).

### V9. Sampler statistics (FACT, verified, 40 000 scenes)
* Marginals of the three sequentially placed points are indistinguishable from $U(0,\pi)$: KS $p=0.41/0.92/0.12$; path 0 vs path 2: $p=0.68$. So the sequential-rejection sampler does **not** create a measurable position bias between "strongest" and "weakest" paths. (I retract an earlier suspicion of an ordering bias: not supported by data.)
* The angle is uniform in $\psi,\phi$, not in $\cos$: 29.1 % of paths have $|\cos\psi|>0.9$ (vs 10 % if uniform in $\cos$). The spatial-frequency density $\propto1/\sqrt{1-(u/\pi)^2}$ is therefore concentrated at the edges of the beam grid ($u\to\pm\pi$, i.e. cells near 8). Inherited from the original generator; same for the official test set. Not a bug (FACT), but an important non-uniformity (CONCERN for interpretation: the test set is edge-heavy in spatial frequency).
* Pairs closer than one DFT beamwidth ($2\pi/16$) in both $u$ and $v$ occur in 0.018 % of scenes (0.006 % of pairs) - negligible.

### V10. Original generator draws alpha before points; v2 draws points before alpha (FACT)
`validation_data_generator` (`tvt_data_generation_v3.py:495-503`, `dldoa_dataset_generation.py:551-560`) draws $\alpha$ first, sorts descending, then `generate_points`. v2 `sample_paths` draws `generate_points` first, then $\alpha$ (`iabr2_sim.py:34-36`). Distributionally identical (independent draws); but the docstring "Exact original angle sampler" is true for the *algorithm*, not for the *stream*: with the same seed the v2/v1 banks are *different realisations* from the official one. Sample_paths(seed=42) first scene differs from `eval_bank.feat[0]` (checked in `a04_04_eval.py`, see section 3). Stylistic.

## 3. Seed audit

### 3.1 All seeds (FACT, from `MANIFEST.json`, v2 bank `.npz` files and the generation scripts)
* 65 bank entries (56 v1 banks in `MANIFEST.json` + 7 v2 banks + the official seed-42 bank + the dldoa test seed-7 bank) over 58 distinct seeds; exactly 3 seeds are shared (1000, 1015, 2020); manifest seeds equal the seeds stored in each `.npz` (0 mismatches).
* v2 banks: T2_d1 9101, T2_d2 9102, T2_d5 9105, tune_clean 9901, tune_d5 9902, tune_g3 9903, tune_L6 9904.
* Official frozen bank: 42 (original generator). `dldoa_dataset` test: 7 (per Agent 01; not verified by me).
* v2 training: `BatchStream(CFG, seed+17*start)` with `default_rng([seed, worker, urandom(4)])`; non-reproducible by construction, cannot coincide with any bank seed (a 3-element entropy list is a different `SeedSequence` than an int seed).
* Note: the `make_bank` "if exists: return" guard means a bank is never regenerated when its spec changes; the seed stored inside the file is the only provenance (process risk, not math).

### 3.2 Collisions (VERIFIED BUG: seed reuse, low impact)
| Seed | Banks | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 | `gain_g2_snr0`, `gain_g0.5_snr15` (n=500 each, L=3) | **Identical psi, phi, alpha for all 500 scenes; identical uniform draws for gain ripple** (`log|D_r|` ratio $=0.2500000000000002\pm6\times10^{-14}$, i.e. exactly $0.5/2$); **noise realisations correlate 1.0000 with std ratio 0.17783 (theory $10^{-15/20}=0.17783$).** They are the same experiment scaled by SNR and gain amplitude. |
| 1000 | `phase_d0_snr0`, `nuis_p0_snr0`, `nuis_p-10_snr0`, `nuis_p-20_snr0` | Only the *first* scene shares psi/phi (three points of `generate_points(4)` equal those of `generate_points(3)` because rejection sampling is sequential); all later scenes differ (RNG streams diverge: the nuisance builder interleaves extra draws). |
| 1015 | `phase_d0_snr15` and the three `nuis_*_snr15` | Same as above. |

Intra-family `nuis_p{0,-10,-20}` share seeds *by design* ("paired across power": 200 identical scenes and noise, verified `np.array_equal` on psi). This is intentional, not a collision bug.

Other candidates I checked: no bank shares a seed with 42, 7, or any v2 seed; the T2 banks are *unpaired across $\delta$* (9101/9102/9105), so the comparison between $\delta=1,2,5^\circ$ carries independent sampling noise of the order $1/\sqrt{1000}$ per SNR point (INFERENCE from binomial sd; Pd differences below about 1.5 points at Pd~0.9 are not resolvable per SNR).

**Impact of the 2020 collision:** in any gain-sweep curve using both banks (gain 0.5 dB at 15 dB SNR and gain 2 dB at 0 dB SNR), the two points are perfectly correlated in scenes and noise direction; they do not add independent evidence and can move together. Effect on a headline number: low (Confidence: Confirmed bug in the sense of unintended seed reuse; severity Low).

### 3.3 Eval-bank regeneration
All FACT, verified by `scratch/a04_04c_eval.py`:

* **`eval_bank.npz` was generated by the original `validation_data_generator` with seed 42** (`scripts/generate_frozen_banks.py:28-56`). Independent regeneration of all 8000 samples with `DG.validation_data_generator(conds, 1000, seed=42, M=8, sigma=0.07, amps_type='ones')` gives **max |data difference| = 0 and max |feat difference| = 0** (bit-exact). The stored file contains metadata `seed=42`, `L=3`, `SNR in {-10,...,25}`.
* `upsample64(downsample16(eval_data)) == eval_data` with max difference 0.0, so the official 64x64 data is an exact zoom-4 (`order=0`) of a 16x16 observation and `DOWN_IDX` is lossless on it.
* v2 `sample_paths(default_rng(42))` first scene: $\psi=[1.379, 2.191, 3.065]$, official `feat[0,0]` $=[2.469, 1.415, 2.912]$: **not the same realisation** (expected, see V10: draw order differs). So a v2/v1 "seed 42" bank would not reproduce the official bank; conversely the official bank is not part of any v2 stream.
* Feature-to-observation mapping: at 25 dB the least-squares fit of the observed 16x16 data with steering-matrix columns built from `psi=feat[:,0]`, `phi=feat[:,1]` leaves a residual of $3.10\times10^{-3}$ per entry (noise variance $10^{-2.5}=3.16\times10^{-3}$; the small shortfall is the 3 fitted degrees of freedom), whereas with `psi` and `phi` swapped the residual is $0.934$. So the `official` loader mapping `psi=feat[:,0]`, `phi=feat[:,1]` (`notebook_src.py`, `load_bank`) is correct; no AoA/AoD swap.
* Minimum pairwise Euclidean separation in the official bank is $0.52376\ge\pi/6=0.52360$ rad: the rejection constraint is respected.
* Assumption (not verified by me): the `dldoa_dataset/test_*` set (seed 7 per Agent 01) was generated by the same function; `infer_dldoa_test.py:20` asserts the zoom structure, which I verified holds for the eval bank.

## 5. Concerns and limitations (not bugs)

1. **(CONCERN, Low) Seed collision 2020** - see 3.2. Confidence: Confirmed (seed reuse); effect: Low.
2. **(CONCERN, Low) Per-entry SNR** - realised SNR per sample varies (std of signal power 0.99 at L=1). Inherited from the original protocol.
3. **(CONCERN, Low) Angle distribution uniform in angle, not in $\cos$** - 29 % of paths near end-fire; inherited and identical for the official test set.
4. **(CONCERN, Low) Impairment applied to codebooks, noise added after combining.** The noise stays i.i.d. white after the impaired combiners. In a physical receiver, per-antenna gain error before combining would colour the noise ($\mathrm{diag}(g_r)$-weighted). Not a bug relative to the stated model (same model as the v1 suite). Confidence: Possible issue for realism only.
5. **(CONCERN, Low) T2 banks unpaired across $\delta$**, so cross-$\delta$ deltas include sampling noise.
6. **(CONCERN, Low) `make_bank` skip-if-exists** - a bank built with older parameters would be silently reused; the stored `phase_deg`, `gain_db`, `seed` provide provenance but nothing checks them.
7. **(CONCERN, Low) Heat-target cell collisions** at high $L$ (up to 7.4 % of scenes at $L=7$) yield fewer positives than paths.
8. **(CONCERN, Low) Test-set-informed design** (`notebook_src.py:104-118`, `:671`): design diagnostics computed on the official seed-42 test set. This is a methodology issue for Agent (leakage of test information into model design), not a generation bug; insufficient evidence from my side to say how far it influenced final choices.

Truncation of `psi`/`phi` to float32 in bank files changes angles by $<10^{-7}$ rad (FACT, negligible vs 0.5-cell $\approx0.2$ rad).

## 6. Conclusion

The dataset-generation pipeline is mathematically consistent with the original DL-DOA generator and with the stated physical model. The only verified defect is unintended seed reuse in three v1 bank families (one of which - seed 2020 - produces two perfectly correlated banks). All other suspicions were checked and cleared or downgraded to inherited protocol properties.
