## 1. What the authors did (one paragraph, before any criticism)

The base paper (Lloria et al., IEEE TVT 2026, vol. 75 no. 6, p. 10206) trains U-Net/ResNet on an "infinite" on-the-fly dataset (SNR -10..25 dB, L = 1..10, alpha ~ CN(0,1/L), angles uniform on [0, pi], Gaussian targets with sigma 0.07), evaluates with 1000 realizations per SNR and "the number of desired paths is set to L ... for all methods". Its Table I lists only optimizer, learning rate, batch size, epochs, training size (10000) and validation size (1000) (rendered page, `scratch\x3_tableI.png`). The released code (`DL_DOA\src\tvt_data_generation_v3.py`) uses `sep = pi/6`, `randint(1,10)` paths, `randint(-15,25)` dB and draws the per-antenna phase error with the global unseeded `np.random.uniform` inside the per-example loop (lines 70-117 and 491-492), i.e. redrawn for every example. The IABR-v2 project (a) evaluates the authors' official U-Net weights on the authors' own seed-42 test generator (`eval_bank.npz`, bit-exact) and on a second draw (`dldoa_dataset` test, seed 7, bit-exact per agent 01's `a01_regen_seed7.py`), (b) builds its own generator `iabr2_sim.py` (same sampler and same physics, plus a project-added gain error), and (c) trains on a fresh stream `default_rng([seed, worker, os.urandom(4)])` (`iabr2_sim.py:127`, 48,000 steps x 256), with no evaluation, no checkpoint selection and no bank access inside `train()` (`notebook_src.py:701-749`; `train_runner.py` stops at `# [TRAIN-END]`).

## 3. New issues discovered by the panel

1. **Base paper is not named in the v2 notebook (CONCERN, Low-Medium, attribution).** `notebook_src.py` contains 0 matches for "Lloria" and 0 for "TVT"; it credits Meneses-Albala (lines 7, 1017) and the repository URL (line 8). "The authors' official U-Net weights" therefore reads as Meneses' weights, although the model, the training protocol and the seed-42 test generator come from Lloria et al. (TVT 2026). The reproduction module's header (`dldoa_dataset_generation.py:9`) does name Lloria. Whether the thesis text cites Lloria: Insufficient evidence.
2. **The neural network adds nothing on clean banks; the gain over U-Net is the physics decoder (CONCERN, Medium, interpretation and attribution).** NOMP alone (no network) scores 0.7581 vs IABR-v2 0.7551 on the official bank and 0.7564 vs 0.7531 on `dldoa_test`, and "v2 no IABC" 0.7567. The uncited NOMP algorithm (item 17) carries the clean-bank improvement over the U-Net. The network's benefit, if any, is on impaired banks (self-calibration/IABC) and on wall-clock; this panel did not test that. FACT (`outputs\RESULTS.md` table A; `RESULTS_dldoa_test.md`).
3. **The Pd floor from the linear ramp is larger than reported in 06 (see row 8).** At delta = 1 deg, 0.56% of components (not 0.13%) are irrecoverably wrong when the endfire fold is counted, because the ramp pushes $|u|$ past $\pi$ and the component reappears near the opposite endfire. DERIVATION + measured. Severity Medium (interpretation only; hits all methods equally).
4. **Non-wrap near-coincident pairs exist in the generator (row 6).** Rate 6.2e-6 per pair under the L=2 law; 0 in the 8000-sample official bank is the expected outcome, not evidence of impossibility. Low.
5. **Validity reference in 06 I2 is inappropriate** (within-bank maximum as yardstick). A null distribution of independent draws (max 0.80-0.89) is the correct reference; the conclusion stands (row 2). None.
6. **DFT-SIC is not the paper's DFT-CEA** (N_DFT 512 vs 1024, different iteration) (row 18). Low.

## 4. Insufficient evidence

- Whether the authors' official U-Net was trained/validated on data overlapping the seed-42 stream (row 13).
- Whether Meneses' Table 2 impairment realizations are drawn per sample or fixed, and their per-cell sample count (row 15).
- Whether the thesis text cites Lloria, NOMP, TSDCE or the focal-loss source (rows 17, item 1).
- The 03 report's 2.7% ramp figure at delta = 5 deg (row 8).
- Sampling rate of non-wrap close pairs at L = 9 (single event in 4,000 scenes).

## 5. Summary judgment

Leakage and validity agree on the essentials: no sample-level or checkpoint-level train/test contact (rows 1, 9), no stale caches (row 12), and only design-level exposure of the official bank (row 10) whose effect is not detectable on an unseen bank at the 0.006-0.008 level. Corrections to 06/07 are matters of precision (rows 2, 4-6, 8, 13, 14). The provenance concerns hold (rows 16-18, new issues 1-2): the physics decoder that produces the gain over the U-Net is uncited, the base paper is not named in the notebook, and the gain-error model is a project assumption.
